Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Expense Tracker collects, uses, and protects your information. By using our app, you agree to this policy.

🔒 Privacy First: Your financial data stays on YOUR device. All transactions, expenses, and budgets are stored locally using Hive database. Sensitive authentication data (like PIN codes) is stored securely using Flutter Secure Storage. We do NOT upload your financial data to any servers.

What Data We Collect

Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Financial Data: Transactions, expenses, income, budgets, categories
  • Security: PIN codes (stored securely using Flutter Secure Storage), biometric authentication preferences
  • Settings: Theme, language, currency, notification preferences

Analytics & Services

  • Firebase Analytics: App usage patterns, screen views, feature usage, crash reports. Firebase Analytics may process technical, device, app-instance, usage, and diagnostic information as necessary to provide analytics services. (NO financial data)
  • AdMob: Advertising ID, device info, ad interactions for displaying ads
  • FCM: Push notification delivery using Firebase Cloud Messaging and the "all_users" topic. We do not maintain a database of individual FCM registration tokens.
Important: We do not send your transaction amounts, expense details, income, or budget information to Firebase, AdMob, or any external services.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Displays ads to support free access. AdMob may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device info, and ad interactions. AdMob does NOT access your financial data.

Opt-out: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of personalization

Firebase Analytics

Tracks app usage patterns, performance, and crashes. We do NOT send your transaction amounts, expense details, or financial data to Analytics.

Firebase Remote Config

Controls app features and ad settings remotely. Does NOT receive your financial data.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

Sends push notifications for app updates and tips. Uses topic-based messaging ("all_users"). We do NOT store individual FCM tokens or send personalized financial notifications.

App Permissions

  • INTERNET: Display ads (financial data never transmitted)
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Budget alerts and reminders (optional)
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Fingerprint/face unlock (uses Android APIs, no biometric data stored)
  • READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Only when you import/export CSV files

Data Retention

Local Data: Retained on your device until you delete it. Removed when app is uninstalled.

Analytics: Firebase retains usage data per their retention policies (typically 14 months).

Ads: AdMob data retained per Google's policies.

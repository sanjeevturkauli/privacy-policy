Privacy Policy for WiFi Smart Tools : Analyzer

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

WiFi Smart Tools : Analyzer ("the App") is developed to help users analyze and manage information about their own WiFi and network connections. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the App.

By using WiFi Smart Tools : Analyzer, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

WiFi Smart Tools : Analyzer is designed to provide network-related tools without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information directly to us.

Depending on the features you use and the Android permissions granted to the App, the App may access certain device and network information required to provide its functionality.

Network Information

The App may access information related to your current network connection, such as:

  • WiFi connection information
  • Network name (SSID), where permitted by Android
  • Network status and connection type
  • IP address or network address information
  • Network speed and connectivity information

This information is used to provide network analysis and diagnostic features within the App.

2. Location and Nearby Device Permissions

Android may require location-related permissions for certain WiFi scanning and network discovery functionality.

If you grant the required permission, the App may use the permission to access WiFi or nearby network information needed for the requested feature.

Important: The App does not intentionally use your location to track your physical movements. Location-related permissions may be required by Android to allow WiFi scanning and related functionality.

You can manage or revoke permissions at any time through your Android device settings. Some features may not work correctly if the required permissions are denied.

3. Network Speed Testing

The App may provide network speed testing features such as ping, download speed, and upload speed tests.

These tests may communicate with publicly available internet endpoints or servers to measure network performance.

Network requests generated during speed tests are used to calculate performance measurements such as latency, download speed, and upload speed.

4. QR Code and Network Tools

The App may provide QR code or WiFi-related utility features. Information entered or generated through these features is used only to provide the requested functionality.

The App does not require users to create an online account to use these tools.

5. Data Storage

WiFi Smart Tools : Analyzer does not require an online account or cloud account for its core functionality.

Where information is stored locally by the App, such information remains on your device unless you choose to share it or otherwise transfer it using features provided by your device.
